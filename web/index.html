How it works

The agent plays a short “mm-hmm” while you speak. Its real answer uses a separate audio path and always has priority.

  • When: after 2.4 seconds of speech and at least five recent words.
  • When to stop: when you pause, seem about to finish, or the agent needs to answer.
  • Keep it controlled: one acknowledgement task at a time, with a 4.5-second cooldown.

We use a saved audio clip to avoid a speech-generation delay. It never becomes part of the AI’s conversation history. The trade-off is a repeated voice clip and simple pause detection.

How we check it

Eight recordings are replayed with acknowledgements on and off. We compare response delay, acknowledgement delay, cancellations and badly timed audio. Tests cover stopping, interruptions, slow providers and shutdown.

Limit: the full benchmark was recorded before the Windows audio fix. It needs a fresh run. Better naturalness and no added delay are not yet proven.
