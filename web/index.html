Audio first · Python + LiveKit
01 / LISTEN

Read the delivery

A 1.5-second audio window updates every 250 ms, tracking energy, uncertainty and frustration cues.

Delivery cues, not a diagnosis of emotion.
02 / ACKNOWLEDGE

A little “mm-hmm”

Short, saved clips acknowledge longer speech. Strong frustration cues mean fewer acknowledgements and no “go on.”

Stops when you pause or an answer begins.
03 / RESPOND

Keep the answer moving

The answer runs independently. Acoustic work uses a small queue and drops old windows to avoid a backlog.

The answer never waits for acoustic inference.
TESTED, WITH LIMITS

12 cloud calls. 6 paired comparisons.

All calls completed. Audio analysis took 0.76 ms P50; answers averaged 299 ms slower with it on. More trials are needed to explain the difference.
